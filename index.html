Prologue · Where it started

Why I love this work

In 2013, when I was a kid, my father bought me my first computer. I didn’t even know what a computer was. I kept poking around in it and figuring things out on my own, and I’ve loved tech ever since.

Now I love building anything: content, websites, software, models. Building and creating is what keeps me going.

In 2023 I stopped only watching and started making. I filmed, edited and posted a shoe unboxing Short on my own channel. It passed 384K views, and three years later new people still find it every week.

That hooked me. Since then I’ve designed thumbnails, run live streams for another creator, built Tableau dashboards and built websites with AI, including this one.

I’m looking for marketing, communications and content roles in the Greater Toronto Area.

Brampton, Ontario, Canada · Permanent resident
